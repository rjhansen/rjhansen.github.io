I was born in a small Iowa hospital during a terrible snowstorm.
I grew up on a farm in Hardin County, Iowa, and as soon as I could
escaped for the big city. After living in Houston and Silicon Valley
I finally landed in northern Virginia — close enough to Yankee lands
to have enlightened social policy, far enough below the Mason-Dixon
line to have authentic stir-fried Southern craziness.

I hold undergraduate and graduate degrees in computer science. I
was invited to speak on a creation of mine, language-theoretic
security, at Black Hat USA, and I may have been the first DEF CON
speaker to warn about the dangers of electronic voting.
