## Disambiguation

There are at least two different Robert Hansens in the computer
security community. I’m Robert J. Hansen, co-inventor of
[language theoretic security](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Language-theoretic_security),
speaker at Black Hat USA, DEF CON, and OSCON. I’m not
[Robert “RSnake” Hansen](https://rsnake.com/about.html), who has
also spoken at Black Hat USA and DEF CON. From all I hear he’s a
nice guy and I wish him well, but I’m not him.

In person, I’m just Rob. If there’s any possibility of confusion,
please use my full name with initial.
